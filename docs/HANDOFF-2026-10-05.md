### 1Sat Ordnance (game guns as 1Sat ordinals)
- **The store**, `/1satordnance/store`:
  - 22 guns; prices are per rarity in `PRICE_SATS` (`src/lib/ordnance.ts`).
  - Buying = one wallet transaction: the inscription goes to the buyer, the price goes to the house.
  - A purchase counts only if `/api/ordnance/issued` verifies the payment on chain.
- **Every gun has its own 3D model.** 18 are CC BY models from Sketchfab (credits in `docs/ordnance-models.md`), and each of the 4 stock models is used once.
- **Poster cards:** a recruitment-poster card per gun, pre-rendered into `public/ordnance/cards/`. Re-render them with the dev tool `/1satordnance/render` under `next dev`.
- **On the cards:**
  - **Hover / tap:** a live 3D swivel.
  - **Click / hold:** test-fire with the gun's own ammo (a demo; nothing goes on chain).
  - **Keyboard:** Space or Enter.
- **One page and share image per gun:** `/1satordnance/store/<slug>`, e.g. `/big-blocker`.
- **After a purchase:** a screen with play, share-on-X and view-on-chain buttons.
- **Ammo tokens** (`src/lib/ammo.ts`): 9MM, SHELLS, BEAM, CELLS, RPG, NADES; the PNEE Shotgun takes **real PNEE** (`1599c4e4…5ae4_0`).
  - **Status:** not minted yet. Until each type is minted, guns accept any token.
  - **Owner tool** (unlinked): `/1satordnance/ammo/mint?qbranch=1`. Inscribe the icon, deploy, paste the token id into `ammo.ts`, then list packs as OrdLock v2.
  - **Store:** `/1satordnance/ammo` buys listings through the player's wallet; the house address receives the payment.
- **For wallets:** `/api/ordnance/manifest` publishes model, tint, tintAmount, flip, roll, spin and ammo per gun, and bWalletX's 3D cabinet reads it. Models and art are CORS-open.

2. **Mint the ammo** from your wallet at `/1satordnance/ammo/mint?qbranch=1`, then list packs. Two wallet behaviours are untested:
   - a 0-sat placeholder output;
   - spending the funding output in a second approval.

   - **Entry:** about $0.10 per player.
   - **Payout:** coins are shares of the pot, paid out by a server key (`POT_WIF`, which you create and fund yourself).
