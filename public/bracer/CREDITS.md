# bRacer credits

bRacer ships no third-party art, models, textures or HDRIs. Everything on screen is generated in code
(src/lib/hyper/*): the circuits (3D splines), ships (lathe + extrusion), track and city textures (canvas),
sky (shader), signage (canvas, an original homage to 90s electronic-label graphic design) and the sound
effects (Web Audio). No HexGL or WipEout code, assets or names are used.

Soundtrack: the site's existing tracks, credited in public/music/CREDITS.md and in the in-game now-playing chip.
Fonts (next/font): Barlow Condensed, Space Mono, Noto Sans JP, all SIL Open Font License, via Google Fonts.
