Processing done here: textures resized and re-encoded to WebP.

No third-party 3D models are used. The rally cars (lofted bodywork, wheels, livery), trees, palms, cacti, rocks and grass are generated in code (`src/lib/rally/carBuild.ts`, `plants.ts`) so there is nothing to license. The livery sponsors are parodies, not real brands. An earlier version used Kenney's CC0 Car Kit and Nature Kit; they were replaced for fidelity and removed.
