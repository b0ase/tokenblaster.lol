# Dr Kweg S Wong (Act 2 escort)

- `hi.glb` (37k tris, 1024 WebP) and `lo.glb` (9.2k tris, 512 WebP): Tripo image_to_model + animate_rig (Mixamo spec) from the kwegwong.com cartoon (`kwegwong/public/kweg_adventures.png`), owned by b0ase. Rest pose rebaked to identity bone rotations so the shared Quaternius clip library (`public/gallery/erobots/mixamo-anims.glb`) drives it; simplified and meshopt compressed with glTF-Transform.
